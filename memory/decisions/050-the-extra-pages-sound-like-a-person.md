# The extra pages sound like a person

Chosen 2026-09-12 by the owner: make the public pages read as human writing,
no machine phrasing, no em dashes, and no numbers invented for effect.

## What was checked first

Current writing on what reads as machine-written, rather than going from
memory. The tells that mattered here: abstract filler and unearned
intensifiers, the not-X-but-Y contrast, oddly precise figures nobody counted,
and a steady unvarying rhythm. Two useful lists were
[Grammarly's](https://www.grammarly.com/blog/ai/common-ai-words/) and
[Forbes' May 2026 update](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jodiecook/2026/05/21/15-new-giveaway-signs-of-ai-writing-may-2026-update/),
which names "actually", "real", "quietly" and "the work" as current tells.

Em dashes were already absent. That rule has held since the start.

## What was cut, and why

**Startup phrasing.** "Three steps, no friction" is now "Getting started".
"Two roles, no hierarchy theater" is now "Who does what". Neither of the
originals said anything a reader needed.

**Metaphors reached for twice.** "You hold the pen" appeared on two pages.
One is now "Nothing publishes itself", the other "you decide at every one of
them". "Originals are sacred" is "Your original is always there".

**Numbers invented for effect.** "Thirty half-finished notebooks" counted
nothing: no class was measured. "The two to four topics worth revisiting" was
a range pulled from nowhere, and is now "a short list of topics". The numbers
that stayed are the ones that are true: six characters in a class code, four
steps in the loop.

**The not-X-but-Y contrast.** "Built from the shared notes, not from thin
air" is now "Made out of the notes your class already shared, so the questions
are about your course", which says the useful half and drops the strawman.
"Checked by people rather than guesswork" is now "someone has read every line
of it".

**"Actually" as an intensifier**, twice, both gone. So are the flourish
absolutes: "forever", "permanently", and one of the two "nothing changes
silently"s.

**Internal words on a page for strangers.** "Cut where you are, not in UTC"
is "Days are cut in your own time zone". The eyebrow "The melt, start to
finish" is "Start to finish": the melt is our word, not a visitor's.

## What was left alone

The privacy page. It was already the most human writing in the repository,
concrete and unhedged, down to "your note leaves the database for nowhere".
Rewriting it would only have made it worse.

Also left: "Typos, fragments, half-ideas: all welcome. Formatting is not your
job.", "Sources welcome.", "regenerate the class code when it leaks". Those
already sound like somebody talking.

The rule is in `CLAUDE.md` now so it does not drift back.
