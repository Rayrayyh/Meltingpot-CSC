# 055 The submission film is a rebuilt, light-mode walkthrough

Summary: On 2026-10-05 the owner asked for a brand-new 2:00 light-mode film after Motionfly V2 and Cua Spaces, with less zoom, more typing and clicking, the Tabbit film's own typing and click sounds, and the landing film's story plus the AI; it lives in docs/videos/walkthrough, rebuilds the app's screens in HTML from the app's own tokens and words on the Biology 101 seed data, and replaces the screenshot demo of decision 054 as the submission video.

## What was decided (2026-10-05)

- The owner's words and answers are in `docs/videos/walkthrough/notes/DECISIONS.md`. Two of them carry risk and are
  the owner's call: the typing and click sounds are lifted from another product's launch film ("Lift the exact
  samples"), so they are not committed and must be credited or replaced if that company objects; and the screens are
  recreated rather than recorded, which the end card and the Devpost disclosure both say.
- The class is Biology 101, not the landing film's AP Calculus BC, because every AI screen the story needs exists in the
  app's recorded run for Biology 101 and none exists for calculus.
- The look follows a measured style sheet (`notes/STYLE.md`): cream stage, a centred window with icon-and-keyword
  captions, word-by-word headlines with one orange word, props around the window, no zoom transitions and one gentle
  pull back. The four chunks were built in parallel on one kit (`assets/film.js`), reviewed through four lenses with a
  skeptic per lens, fixed, and checked again on the final render.
- `docs/videos/demo` stays as the earlier screenshot cut.
